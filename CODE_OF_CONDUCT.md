# Code of conduct

We want shipcli to be a welcoming, constructive project for everyone.

## Our standards

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion
on the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and other
conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the
contact options on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/lackim). Reports
will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
comments, and issues, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is
representing the project.
